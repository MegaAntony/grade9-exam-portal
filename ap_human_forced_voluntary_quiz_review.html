Asylum seekers from Syria in Europe

VH926950 Skill 2.C ✓ 1 / 1 (Selected: C)

There has been an increase in asylum seekers from Syria to the European Union in recent years. Which of the following best describes the reason for this migration?

(A) A large rural-to-urban population shift within Syria
(B) A continuing rise in employment for asylum seekers in the European Union
✓ (C) A continuing issue of conflict and political unrest within Syria [Correct & Antony's Choice]
(D) The tightening of asylum policies by the European Union
(E) The tightening of guest-worker policies by the European Union
💡 Pedagogical Deconstruction:

Civil war and acute political conflicts in Syria produced catastrophic displacement, compelling civilians to flee across borders and apply for international asylum.

⚠️ AP Trap Alert: Intervening Obstacles vs. Push Drivers

The Trap: Distractors (D) and (E) describe the tightening of asylum policies. Tightening policies is an intervening obstacle / border restriction, not a root cause driving migration.

European Union and asylum seekers (Step Migration)

VH930370 Skill 2.C ❌ 0 / 1 (Selected: A)

A refugee enters the European Union in Italy and is temporarily housed in Germany. He is then granted asylum and is permanently settled in Sweden. Which of the following describes this type of migration that is facilitated by the European Union’s open border policies?

✗ (A) Chain migration within the refugee’s family and social network [Antony's Choice — Incorrect: Chain migration requires following family/relatives who migrated previously]
(B) Guest worker migration based upon the free movement of labor
(C) As an internally displaced person within a single state
✓ (D) Step migration across member states to a final destination [Official Correct Answer — Moving through sequential waypoints: Italy → Germany → Sweden]
(E) As a rural-to-urban migrant seeking employment opportunities
💡 Pedagogical Deconstruction:

Step migration occurs in a series of shorter stages or stops toward a final destination. Traveling from Italy to Germany and then to Sweden illustrates step migration across European Union member states.

⚠️ AP Trap Alert: Chain Migration vs. Step Migration Trap

The Trap: Antony selected (A) Chain migration because students frequently associate refugees with community networks. However, the stem mentions zero relatives or family links.

The Reality & Rule of Thumb:
• Step Migration: Movement in a sequential series of stages (Point A $\rightarrow$ Point B $\rightarrow$ Point C).
• Chain Migration: Following family members or members of the same ethnic group who migrated earlier.

South Asian migrants to Persian Gulf

VH916977 Skill 2.C ✓ 1 / 1 (Selected: A)

Which term best describes the majority of voluntary migrants from South Asia to the Persian Gulf countries of Bahrain, Qatar, and Kuwait during the 1990s and 2000s?

✓ (A) Guest workers [Correct & Antony's Choice]
(B) Refugees
(C) Rural-to-urban migrants
(D) Internally displaced persons
(E) Asylum seekers
💡 Pedagogical Deconstruction:

Though hundreds of thousands of South Asians work in Persian Gulf countries, a majority do not have permanent legal residency or pathways to citizenship. They are guest workers under temporary employer-sponsored labor visas.

⚠️ AP Trap Alert: Guest Worker Pyramid & Remittance Impact

The Reality & Rule of Thumb:
• Persian Gulf guest workers create an extreme male sex bulge in the 20–45 age cohort on population pyramids.
• They generate immense international remittance capital flows back to India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and the Philippines.
