### Question 2: European Union and asylum seekers (Step Migration)
* **College Board Reference**: `VH930370`
* **Topic**: `Topic 2.11: Forced and Voluntary Migration`

* **Stimulus**: Multistage Migration Scenario (Italy $\rightarrow$ Germany $\rightarrow$ Sweden)

**Question Stem:**
> A refugee enters the European Union in Italy and is temporarily housed in Germany. He is then granted asylum and is permanently settled in Sweden. Which of the following describes this type of migration that is facilitated by the European Union’s open border policies?

**Answer Choices:**
* [x] **(A) Chain migration within the refugee’s family and social network** *(❌ Selected by Antony — INCORRECT)*
* [ ] (B) Guest worker migration based upon the free movement of labor
* [ ] (C) As an internally displaced person within a single state
* [ ] **(D) Step migration across member states to a final destination** *(✅ OFFICIAL CORRECT ANSWER)*
* [ ] (E) As a rural-to-urban migrant seeking employment opportunities

> [!WARNING]
> **AP Trap Alert on Question 2 (Chain Migration vs. Step Migration Trap)**:
> * **Why Antony and Many AP Students Fall Into This**: Antony saw the refugee context and associated it with family/social networks, selecting **(A) Chain migration**.
> * **The Fatal Distractor Flaw in (A)**:
>   * **Chain migration** occurs when a migrant moves to a specific location *because relatives or members of the same nationality previously migrated there* (e.g., an uncle settles in Chicago and sponsors cousins and siblings to join him). The question stem makes **zero mention** of family members, relatives, or ethnic enclaves!
> * **Why (D) Step Migration is the Correct Concept**:
>   * **Step Migration (E.G. Ravenstein's Laws)**: Long-distance migration that occurs in a **series of shorter stages, steps, or intermediate waypoints** toward a final preferred destination.
>   * In this scenario: **Stage 1 (Entry)**: Crosses Mediterranean into Italy $\rightarrow$ **Stage 2 (Transit / Temporary Shelter)**: Housed in Germany $\rightarrow$ **Stage 3 (Final Settlement)**: Granted legal asylum in Sweden.
>   * The EU's Schengen Area open-border agreement eliminates passport controls, directly facilitating step-by-step movement across member states.
> * **Exam Day Rule**:
>   * Following relatives / social networks = **CHAIN MIGRATION**
>   * Moving through intermediate stops (Point A $\rightarrow$ Point B $\rightarrow$ Point C) = **STEP MIGRATION**
